A notebook has a different pace from a paper. It can hold a question before it has an answer, a calculation before it becomes an argument, or a useful figure before there is a story to put around it.

## A useful kind of incompleteness

An unfinished note is easier to revisit when it says what is known and what is still uncertain. The rough edge is part of the record.

> Write down the question while you can still remember why it was interesting.

## Small enough to keep

A page, a diagram, a few equations. Give the idea a title, leave a date, and move on. Connections can come later.
